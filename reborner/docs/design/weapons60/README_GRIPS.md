## ชิ้นที่ใช้ได้แต่มีตำหนิ (DONE-FLAW)

- **bl08 เคียวโซ่พายุ** — left hand does not hold the chain end (chain crosses the body and exits the right edge of the frame); chain color changes gold to silver mid-way; handle collar slightly separated from the grip
- **bt01 ไม้เบสบอลไม้ร้าว** — only ONE hand actually grips the handle; the other forearm crosses in front and the bat knob pokes out beside it ungripped — fails the two-hand spec, needs a fix or re-draw by Claude
- **bt08 สารานุกรมเล่มสุดท้าย** — both hands support the book correctly, but the book is half-open (reads as a casting pose, not a closed blunt weapon) and the gold aura halos around the whole book instead of only the spine/page edges
- **pt01 ดินสอ** — a second vertical pencil crosses the hand behind the horizontal pencil (extra object); facing left, matches master, horizontal pencil gripped correctly
- **pt08 หอกดินสอ** — both hands grip the pencil shaft correctly and energy lines spiral around it, but the spear angle is steeper than spec — tip rises well above head instead of at face height
- **pt09 เข็มน้ำแข็ง** — right hand grips the ice needle correctly with runes and tip sparkle, but the needle angles up-forward instead of the specified down-forward angle
- **rg05 พลุกระดาษงานวัด** — both hands hold the tube correctly and the flame is festive orange, but the tube angles much steeper than the specified 45 degrees (near-vertical) and the flame sits at the top fuse rather than hugging the tube
- **dv01 คีมช่างซ่อม** — right hand grips the pliers correctly and the metal is worn and dull, but the jaws point forward/up instead of the specified down-forward angle
- **dv05 โดรนส่งของ** — pose matches spec (pointing right hand clean, open left palm) but the drone hovers slightly above the palm instead of resting on it; only the front rotor pair is clearly visible (side view)
